## Accepted progress

- Nuke ten-player performance is an enormous improvement: roughly 200-300 FPS,
  responsive through the owner's session, without notable low-FPS moments.
  Nuke lighting looked good without a noticed lighting bug.
- Old Dust II performance and lighting were very good, with no complaints.
- Inferno is mostly a victory: lighting across the map, church and T apartments
  is improved; removing fake fountain water was accepted. Apartment access was
  exercised, although trim lighting defects remain.
- Cache remake generally looks good and its squeaky door opens correctly.
  Nuke doors open and produce the expected squeak. Nuke vent covers break.
- Old Inferno looks very good overall. Minor lighting blemishes were expressly
  deferred. A brief roughly 500-FPS reading followed by roughly 200 FPS was
  acceptable to the owner for now.
- Mirage is broadly promising and roughly 100-300 FPS, somewhat unstable but
  good enough for now. Named traversal/material defects remain.
- Grounded weapon motion, looking around and utility hand motion are much
  better across the tested weapons. Retain this accepted motion.
- Decoy bounce/contact sounds work again.

Further general FPS optimization is now **low priority**. Preserve the accepted
renderer and responsiveness. Making every core map playable with ten players
and acceptable performance remains **high priority**; this is not a claim that
every map or the old long-term FPS aspiration has been completed.

## High: movement and viewmodel finish

- Walking speed feels too fast. Initial crouch speed seemed reasonable, but
  the owner later also reported crouch walking too fast, especially backward
  on Mirage. Record both observations; do not dismiss the later correction.
  Measure original movement, directional input and stance transitions before
  changing shared speed coefficients. Preserve the accepted responsive feel.
- In the air, viewmodel motion appears frozen at the last grounded pose.
  Match original jump/air/landing response: the weapon should respond to the
  jump, move through the landing and settle, without teleporting. Keep the
  accepted grounded motion across guns and utility. This finishing work is high
  priority because the rest is already close.
