# October 16 owner playtest and priorities

Owner feedback on the frozen `9cabef2` release candidate (source tree
`14082bcb69f66898987f2912b669564452331276`, merged through #208; checkpoint
#209). These are owner observations and requested outcomes, not newly verified
fixes. The October 14 acceptance ledger remains intact. Preserve the frozen
desktop candidate and owner settings until a replacement passes verification.

## Direction

**Sustained gameplay performance is priority one.** The immediate goal is a
playable game: high, predictable frame rates and correct input, movement,
weapons and map interactions. Content-pack expansion is deferred. Do not
interpret working components or isolated screenshots as whole-game acceptance.

Owner reports roughly 80 FPS with jitter on old Dust II with bots, versus
200–300 FPS alone in the console/range test. Native expectation on this PC is
roughly 300–500 FPS, with about 500 FPS desired on Dust II. These are owner
reports/targets, not controlled paired measurements or promised results.
Nuke initially performs better and degrades during play. Mouse response feels
fine when frames are high. Compare fixed map/camera/resolution/settings, solo
versus bots and early versus sustained play; record CPU/GPU frame distributions,
entity/resource growth and actual presentation behavior. Preserve visual fidelity.

The owner explicitly requests more than four parallel agents. Use separate
worktrees for implementation and coordinate heavy builds/native/render access;
do not confuse a tool concurrency cap with the machine's hardware capacity.

## Owner-approved exceptions to the historical baseline

- Grenade launch trajectories and throwing feel should target **CS2 feel**,
  explicitly superseding the previous CS:GO-only behavior rule for this scope.
  This authorizes clean-room observation/public documentation, not copying
  Source SDK/leaked code or shipping CS2 assets/UI. Other content remains old
  CS:GO. Preserve authoritative TickInput/content-pack seams.
- Automatically read and translate the player's **CS2 config/keybinds** during
  setup where available. Source config is read-only; preserve existing rewrite
  preferences and unsupported bindings. No manual config copying or terminal.
- Sensitivity 1 should be the default for new settings and must match CS mouse
  response exactly. Do not overwrite the owner's existing sensitivity.
- Master volume 1 should be a reasonable, quieter output; owner currently finds
  about 0.15 comfortable and 1 painfully loud. Above-1 gain may be supported.
- The extra center fire-mode notice is unnecessary; the correct bottom-right
  original HUD indicator is sufficient.
- Molotov rag physics is optional and the absolute lowest priority; the owner
  recognizes it may be a CS2-only detail. Do not let it displace playability.

## Execution and acceptance

Parallel tracks: renderer/performance; bot/simulation performance; menu lifecycle;
input/import; viewmodel/animation; weapon timing/AWP; HUD/reference measurement;
map interactions. Root coordinates grenade/decoy, audio/impact and integration.
Use evidence-driven fixes and measure sustained real gameplay. Do not replace
the owner test candidate mid-feedback, claim 500 FPS from a headless/screenshot
test, sacrifice fidelity to hide cost, or count a proposed fix as accepted.

Run required source checks and inspect actual visuals before merge. Keep every
remaining feedback item visible even when a bounded fix lands. Original
attribution, private-only assets and clean-room provenance remain mandatory.
